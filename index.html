About me

I've been working with data since 2018, mostly for large international companies. I started out in SAP BW data warehousing, and today, as a data analyst, I combine SQL, Tableau and conversations with the business into reports that people actually base decisions on.

My favourite moment is when a vague business need turns into a concrete metric and working logic. I've worked on loan approval processes for a German bank, parts procurement at Volkswagen and HVAC sales at Daikin. I make sure the numbers from different systems add up, because a dashboard is only as good as the data underneath it.

Day to day I work with Oracle SQL (CTEs, window functions), Tableau, SAP BusinessObjects and SAP BW on HANA, and I'm currently learning dbt and Power BI. I work comfortably in German and English. I studied Quantitative Methods at the Warsaw School of Economics and Economics in Freiburg.
